MICROBIOLOGY

 Cultures

             o All positive Blood Culture results, as determined by Gram stain, NANOSPHERE Blood Culture, PNA FISH
                   Candida, and/or culture growth.

             o CSF or corneal scrapings showing any growth, including bacterial or fungal.

 Blood Malarial Smear - Any positive result.

 Gram Stains - All CSF and corneal scrapings, lower respiratory smears consistent with pneumonia (many WBCs and
